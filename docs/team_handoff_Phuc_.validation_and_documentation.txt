Documented prioritization rules (Documentation)
----------------------------------------------
- No invented severity scores: The worklist strictly uses official KEV fields and
  never fabricates CVSS or custom risk scores absent from CISA KEV[cite: 1].
- Primary sort (Overdue status): Overdue records (days_until_due < 0) appear first,
  ordered from most overdue (most negative offset) to least overdue[cite: 1].
- Secondary sort (Future deadlines): Non-overdue records follow in chronological
  order by earliest dueDate first, with any missing dueDate placed last[cite: 1].
- Tie-breaking: Identical dueDate records sort by oldest dateAdded first (missing
  dates last), then preserve original input order for deterministic output.

Documented limitations and risk interpretation (Documentation)
--------------------------------------------------------------
- Counts do not equal organizational risk: High vendor/product counts (e.g.,
  Microsoft: 390, Cisco: 101, Apple: 95; Windows: 173, Multiple Products: 86;
  vendor std_dev: 25.44, product std_dev: 7.97) reflect catalog presence, not an
  organization's actual risk exposure or asset inventory[cite: 1].
- Overdue status meaning: Overdue reflects the catalog deadline relative to the
  reference date (2026-10-18), not proof that local systems remain unpatched[cite: 1].
- Ransomware field meaning: "Unknown" means unconfirmed ransomware use, not
  confirmed absence of ransomware threat[cite: 1].
- Defensive boundary: Strictly defensive catalog analysis; no target scanning or
  vulnerability exploitation is performed[cite: 1].
